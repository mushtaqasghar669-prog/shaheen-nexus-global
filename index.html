SIMPLE WORKFLOW

Three steps.

The product is intentionally designed around a simple mental model.

1️⃣

Give it information

Upload a supported document or paste text, a URL, or a note.

2️⃣

Understand it

Shaheen extracts useful information and highlights things that may require attention.

3️⃣

Act on it

Turn important findings into tasks, notes, checklists and saved projects.

ORIGINAL INFORMATION

Useful guides, not filler.

Public informational content gives the website value beyond the private workspace.

How to review an important document

Check dates, obligations, amounts, missing information, required documents and next actions before submitting anything.

How to organize a document-heavy project

Keep the original document, important notes, action checklist, deadlines and completed tasks connected.

How to avoid missing deadlines

Extract dates early, convert them into explicit tasks and keep the source document available for verification.

Why document automation needs human review

Automated extraction can help with organization, but important legal, financial, medical or academic decisions should still be verified against the original source.

SECURITY & PRIVACY

Your files should remain yours.

Supported browser-side document processing happens locally in the browser. Shaheen does not pretend that browser-side processing is equivalent to enterprise encryption or professional legal/medical confidentiality.

Firebase account information is used for authentication and, when Firestore is enabled, workspace synchronization.

Never upload passwords, private keys, payment credentials or other secrets into documents merely for analysis.
