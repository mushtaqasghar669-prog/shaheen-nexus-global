How to organize a busy day

Choose three priorities, capture everything else, and keep one next action visible for each active project.

How to research without losing sources

Record the question, source, useful finding and next action. This makes research reusable instead of becoming a pile of tabs.

How to reduce digital clutter

Capture first, review later, and delete or archive information that no longer helps you make a decision.

About Shine Nexus Global

Shine Nexus Global is a practical digital workspace built around a simple problem: important information is scattered across notes, browser tabs and to-do lists. The first release focuses on useful features that work directly in the browser rather than showing unfinished integrations.
